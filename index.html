🔋 How Energy & Mood Work

⚡ Energy System

  • 100% → Fresh start, bright animations
  • 80-99% → High energy, fast movements
  • 35-79% → Normal energy, standard speed
  • 15-34% → Low energy, slower & dimmer
  • 0-14% → Exhausted, very sleepy

😊 Mood Tracking

  • Focused 🎯 → Productive sites + good energy
  • Excited 😄 → Entertainment & social media
  • Happy 😊 → Balanced browsing habits
  • Tired 😪 → Low energy from long sessions
  • Distracted 😵 → Too much time wasting

📊 What Affects Them

  • Time spent → Energy decreases over time
  • Site type → Productive vs distractive
  • Activity → Mouse/keyboard movement
  • Breaks → Inactivity helps recovery
  • Patterns → Long sessions drain faster

🎮 Pet Changes

  • Animation speed → Slower when tired
  • Brightness → Dims with low energy
  • Reactions → Less enthusiastic when drained
  • Auto-states → Sleepy, focused, bored
  • Colors → Vibrant when energetic

💡 Try it: Watch your pet's energy and mood change as you browse different sites. The longer you stay active, the more tired your pet becomes!
